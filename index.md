Hi! My name is João Henrique Lírio da Silva. I am originally from Divinópolis, Minas Gerais, Brazil.

I completed my PhD in Mathematics at the [Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ)](https://ufrj.br/) in 2026, under the supervision of Professor [Isaia Nisoli](http://lattes.cnpq.br/9060375863016440), with the thesis "Computational Approaches to the Study of Chaotic Dynamical Systems." I am currently a substitute professor at UFRJ.

Previously, I completed my master's degree in Mathematics at the [Federal University of Itajubá (UNIFEI)](https://unifei.edu.br/), under the supervision of Professor [Antonio Carlos Fernandes](http://lattes.cnpq.br/7334805996142405), and my bachelor's degree in [Mathematics Teaching at the Federal Institute of Minas Gerais (IFMG), Formiga campus](https://www.formiga.ifmg.edu.br/).

I develop research in ergodic theory and rigorous computational approaches, with scientific interests in celestial mechanics, the n-body problem, chaotic dynamical systems, perturbation theory, and topics in probability.
