Hi! My name is João Henrique Lírio da Silva. I am originally from Divinópolis, Minas Gerais, Brazil.

I completed my PhD in Mathematics at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ) in 2026, under the supervision of Professor Isaia Nisoli, with the thesis "Computational Approaches to the Study of Chaotic Dynamical Systems." I am currently a substitute professor at UFRJ.

Previously, I completed my master's degree in Mathematics at the Federal University of Itajubá (UNIFEI), under the supervision of Professor Antonio Carlos Fernandes, and my bachelor's degree in Mathematics Teaching at the Federal Institute of Minas Gerais (IFMG), Formiga campus.

I develop research in ergodic theory and rigorous computational approaches, with scientific interests in celestial mechanics, the n-body problem, chaotic dynamical systems, perturbation theory, and topics in probability.

My CV can be found here and my email address is joaolopes350@gmail.com.
